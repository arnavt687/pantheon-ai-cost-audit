# Letter of Intent (non-binding)

**Between:** [Company name] ("Company")
**And:** Pantheon Solutions, represented by Arnav Thakur ("Pantheon")
**Date:** [date]

## 1. Purpose

Company is interested in piloting Pantheon's AI Cost Audit. The audit reviews Company's large-language-model API usage (OpenAI, Anthropic or similar) and recommends ways to lower that spend without hurting product quality.

## 2. Pilot scope

- Company shares a usage summary or a usage export from its provider dashboard, covering one recent month. Token counts and model names are enough.
- Company may also share up to 200 sample prompts that it has chosen and scrubbed of personal data, for a side-by-side quality check.
- Pantheon returns a written report within 5 business days of receiving the data. The report estimates monthly savings for each recommendation.
- Company decides which recommendations, if any, to adopt. Pantheon makes no changes to Company's systems.

## 3. Cost

The pilot is free. If the pilot is useful, the parties may discuss a paid engagement later. Neither party is committed to one.

## 4. Data and confidentiality

- Pantheon will not ask for, and Company should not send, API keys, passwords, or end-customer personal data.
- Pantheon will use Company's data only to prepare the report. It will delete the data within 30 days of delivering the report, or sooner if Company asks.
- Pantheon will not name Company or share its figures publicly without Company's written permission.

## 5. Feedback

If the pilot goes ahead, Company agrees to give brief feedback on how useful the report was. A 15-minute call or a short written note is enough.

## 6. Non-binding

This letter records a shared intention only. It creates no legal obligation on either party, apart from the confidentiality commitments in section 4. Either party may end the pilot at any time.
