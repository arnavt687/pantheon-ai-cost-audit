How to read the range

  • Low end counts only the safer changes: newer models of the same class at a lower price (still worth a quick check), caching of prompt parts you've told us are fixed, and Batch for work nobody waits on.
  • High end adds moves to a smaller model and caching we estimated without seeing your prompts. Those are marked needs quality test: we run 50–200 of your real examples through both models and you compare the answers before anything changes.
  • Costs are recomputed from token counts at list prices. Discounts, credits and committed-use deals aren't included.
